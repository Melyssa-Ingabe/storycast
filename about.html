About & Access

StoryCast is a small home for short audio and video stories that everyone can enjoy, whether you see, hear, or read them.

Our mission

Stories should not be locked behind one way of sensing. Every StoryCast story is published with a written version, so nobody is left out because of how they access the web.

Accessibility features

  • Captions

    Video stories include English captions that describe speech and meaningful sounds. Turn them on or off from the player controls.

  • Transcripts

    Every story, audio or video, has a full text transcript on the same page, including descriptions of important sounds.

  • Keyboard navigation

    Everything on the site can be used without a mouse. A skip link at the top of each page jumps straight to the content.

  • Visible focus

    A clear outline shows where you are as you move through links and controls with the Tab key.

  • Readable colors and type

    Text and background colors are chosen for strong contrast, and text can be enlarged without breaking the layout.

  • Structure for screen readers

    Pages use landmarks and a logical heading order, so assistive technology can move around quickly.

How to explore StoryCast

  1. Start on the home page and choose a featured story.
  2. Press Tab to move between links and controls, and Enter to open one.
  3. On a story page, use the player to listen or watch, or read the transcript below it.
  4. Use the related stories at the bottom of each page to keep going.
